Shipwreck Discovery

A VR diving game set on real wrecks sunk as artificial reefs off Destin, Florida. I'm on the team at Lionfish Central, working on gameplay systems and the real-time art pipeline.

Game Developer & 3D Technical Artist · July 2026 to now · Godot 4, GDScript, OpenXR, Blender

What I do on it

  • Retopologized 18 coral growth-stage assets for VR: UVs, PBR bakes at 1K and 2K, Godot-ready GLBs checked by reimport.
  • Built coral growth, planting, placement and collision systems in Godot, and tuned the wildlife balance around them.
  • Baked full wreck interiors down to retopologized, 4K-textured versions that hold up on Quest.
  • Tracked down VR interaction bugs and wrote regression tests so the fixes stay fixed.

Lionfish Reef Heroes

A pixel-art arcade game for Android and iOS. Lionfish have taken the reef; you dive in, spear them and bring the reef back across ten ocean habitats.

Built at AquaticXR with Ian McLennan and Scott Gonnello · Godot 4.5 · Android and iOS · Coming soon

  • Ten habitats with animated marine life, boss fights and collectible gear.
  • Five assignments based on real marine careers: reef survey, coral nursery, turtle rehab and more.
  • One Godot project builds for desktop, Android and iPhone, with automated builds for both phones.
  • Store integration with a server that verifies purchases.

Sector War

Real-time space strategy in the browser: the pace of an .io game with a slimmed-down grand-strategy layer. You never leave the galaxy view.

Built at AquaticXR with Ian McLennan and Scott Gonnello · TypeScript · WebSockets · Node

  • Three modes, from a 20-minute blitz to a two-hour campaign. AI empires each have a personality; there's diplomacy and a Galactic Council.
  • Online multiplayer for up to eight players over WebSockets, on a deterministic engine.
  • 81 automated tests, including a full bot-vs-bot game, plus a headless soak runner and a Python balance model.
  • The whole game also ships as one 8 MB HTML file that runs offline in any browser.
