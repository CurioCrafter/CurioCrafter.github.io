CurioMesh

Free automatic retopology for Blender. One guided panel turns a dense model or photogrammetry scan into clean quads, part by part, and checks every part against the original with a camera from six sides.

My own project · free and open source (GPLv3) · Python · Blender API · Rust core · AI-agent assisted

Problem
Retopology of dense models and scans is slow manual work.
Approach
Pick what the mesh is for (game asset, animation, subdivision, scan cleanup, sculpt base) and press Start. Each part gets its own face count, and any single part can be redone. A native Rust core runs the heavy steps on every CPU core.
Result
A 1.26-million-face scan went from 47 minutes to about 5. 46 GitHub stars, 17 releases, about 279 tests, and CI that checks the Rust core against the Python reference. Zips for every Blender from 3.6 to 5.2 on Windows, Linux and macOS.

Also: LOD sets, normal and colour baking, kept UV seams, and region retopology for redoing just a face or a hand. And by me: Topology Transitions, guided all-quad edge-loop reductions and expansions.

Shipwreck Discovery

A VR diving game set on real wrecks sunk as artificial reefs off Destin, Florida. I work on it full-time at AquaticXR, on gameplay systems and the real-time art pipeline, and I'm credited as a developer on its Steam page alongside Scott Gonnello and Ian McLennan.

Game Developer & 3D Technical Artist · AquaticXR · July 2026 to now · Godot 4 · GDScript · OpenXR · Blender

What I do on it

  • Build and test coral growth, planting, placement and collision systems in Godot, and tune the wildlife balance around them.
  • Retopologize coral growth stages and large shipwreck assets, lay out UVs, bake PBR materials and deliver Godot-ready GLB files that hold up on Quest.
  • Build the seafloor critters for the experimental branch: 37 species, from octopus and mantis shrimp to crabs, lobsters, urchins and sea slugs, each placed by its real Gulf of Mexico habitat. One shader animates every limb, so the whole field runs cheaply on Quest.
  • Track down VR interaction bugs and write regression tests so the fixes stay fixed.
  • Make the trailers and short videos the studio uses to promote the game.

Music Visualizer

Rust · wgpu / DirectX 12 · WGSL · WebGPU · AI-agent assisted

Problem
Ten audio-reactive looks that have to stay detailed at full-screen 4K.
Approach
A real-time analyzer splits the music into kick, snare and hi-hats, tempo, the drop and section energy; each look is one WGSL shader that gives every signal its own job.
Result
About 0.5 ms per frame per shader at 4K. It lives in the Windows tray, and the same shaders run in a WebGPU web player and a Chrome extension.

Clayspace

Blender 5.1.2 · OpenXR · Index and Quest controllers · AI-agent assisted

Problem
Modelling in Blender from inside PC VR, without paid add-ons.
Approach
Hold a trigger to draw a box, sphere, cylinder or Boolean cutter at the controller tip, release to commit. The hand menu stays on the hand that opened it, and every tool runs on Blender's own APIs.
Result
Blender 5.1 usable in the headset, captured here from the headset view.
